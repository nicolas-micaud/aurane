 Pricing FAQ
 How much does ChatBrat cost? Three plans: Basic $4.99/mo, Pro $12.99/mo, and Premium $23.99/mo, each cheaper on yearly billing (save ~17%). ChatBrat is free while in beta, so nothing is charged yet.
 Is there a free version of ChatBrat? Yes. You can chat for free — no card required. Paid plans add more weekly messages, the better BratMind models, richer roleplay, and more creation slots.
 What's the difference between Basic, Pro, and Premium? Basic runs the BratMind model with 1,750 messages a week. Pro upgrades to BratMind 2.0 with 3,000 messages a week, priority responses, and richer conversations (accents, humor, deeper roleplay). Premium is BratMind Advanced with 7,000 messages a week, unlimited creation, early access, and bonus tokens.
 Are message limits daily or weekly? Weekly. You get a pool of messages per week instead of a hard daily cap, so a heavy day never leaves you stuck.
 Can I cancel anytime? Yes. You can cancel anytime and keep access until the end of your billing period. Annual plans save about 17% versus paying monthly.
 Does ChatBrat train on or sell my chats? No. We don't sell your data, and we use no-training / no-retention terms with our AI providers, so your conversations aren't fed into model training.

 Why 'AI that forgets' is the #1 complaint
 Search r/CharacterAI or any AI-companion Discord and the loudest, most-repeated frustration is memory: characters losing names, personalities, and plot after roughly 15–25 messages, and users saying it got worse as the platforms scaled. Replika takes a different route — it can remember more, but the deeper memory features sit behind the paywall. Either way, the thing people actually want (a companion that remembers) is the thing that's hardest to get. That's the gap ChatBrat is built around.

 How persistent memory actually works
 Instead of relying only on a sliding context window that drops your oldest messages, ChatBrat writes durable facts — about your character, about you, about what's happened in the story — into addressable memory slots that persist across sessions. When you come back tomorrow, the character still knows who you are and what happened. It's the difference between a chat that resets and a relationship that accumulates.

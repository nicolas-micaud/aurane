Privacy

Last updated: 25 September 2026.

Who

The playaurane.com website and the Aurane game are published by ninabot Sàrl, Geneva, Switzerland. This page covers the Season 0 waitlist. The game itself (play.playaurane.com) has its own rules, described inside the application.

What the waitlist stores

Your e-mail address (required), a first name or nickname if you give one, the language you chose, the date you signed up, and the source of your visit (UTM parameters in the address and the site you came from, when present) so we know which channels bring players.

The form is protected by Cloudflare Turnstile, which checks that a human is sending it; to do so Cloudflare processes technical data (IP address, browser) under its own policy. We do not receive your IP address in clear.

Why

To write to you when your seat opens in Season 0, then when the game opens to everyone. Nothing else: no advertising, no resale, no matching with other files. Legal basis: your consent (the form's checkbox), under the Swiss FADP and the GDPR.

For how long

Until you unsubscribe, and at the latest one year after the end of Season 0. The data is hosted at Cloudflare (European Union and Switzerland depending on the processing region).

Your rights

Access, rectification, erasure, withdrawal of consent: every message from the list carries an unsubscribe link, and you can also write to the address shown in this site's footer or reply to any message received from the list. You may lodge a complaint with the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner or your country's authority.

Cookies

The site sets no tracking cookie. It remembers your language in your browser's local storage, and Cloudflare Turnstile may set a technical cookie for the duration of the check.
